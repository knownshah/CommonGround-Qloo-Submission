How it works

Three steps from different to decided.

  1. 01Start with what you love.One film, artist, public figure or game per person. Different tastes welcome.
  2. 02Make the trade-offs visible.Qloo connects cultural tastes. CommonGround ranks the same options for each person.
  3. 03Suggestions. Then agreement.Rule an option out, reconsider, and let every person respond. Your group gets the final say.

For your AI agent

Bring your agent to the table.

Your assistant can resolve everyone’s favorites, compare the group and bring proposals, trade-offs and revisions back into your conversation through MCP. Open the app whenever you want the full comparison or each person’s explicit response.
